About RS8

Parts we'd run ourselves.

RS8 Racing brings Taiwan-manufactured performance parts to Philippine riders — sourced, tested, and stocked by people who ride.

Where It Started

From the Taiwan Speed Factory to the local track

RS8 was built around one idea: Filipino riders shouldn't have to guess whether an imported part actually performs. Taiwan has some of the sharpest small-engine and CVT manufacturing in the world, and we made it our job to find the factories worth trusting.

Every product in our line-up — from cam gears to clutch bells — is sourced directly from that supply chain, then checked against the demands of Philippine riding: heat, traffic starts, and the kind of full-throttle runs stock parts weren't built for.

How We Work

Tested before it's trusted

Before a part earns a place on the shelf, it goes through fitment checks and real-world runs. We're not interested in moving boxes — we're interested in parts that hold up when you're back on the road, weeks after installation.

That's also why we stay reachable. If a part doesn't fit your build the way it should, you'll be talking to someone who understands drivetrains, not a script.
